# Recommendations

## Portfolio

Prioritize product families where transition urgency and pricing confidence are both high. These are the families where a product manager can shape a concrete action plan instead of escalating a vague margin concern.

## Pricing

Separate broad list-price moves from targeted price resets. The business-case view estimates margin lift and revenue at risk, then assigns owner and next gate so commercial teams can evaluate impact before execution.

## Obsolescence

Use substitution fit and installed-base dependency together. High substitution fit lowers customer migration friction, while high installed-base dependency increases communication and last-time-buy planning needs.

## NPD

Use NPD gate score as an entry filter, not a final approval. Concepts still need customer proof, supplier qualification, engineering feasibility, and launch enablement before roadmap commitment.

## Data Confidence

Data controls should travel with the recommendation. A portfolio action with weak CRM win-loss coding or sales training evidence should be treated as a watch item until the signal is validated.

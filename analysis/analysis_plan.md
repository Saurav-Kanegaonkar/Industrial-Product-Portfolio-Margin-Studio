# Analysis Plan

## Objective

Build an interview-ready product-management artifact for an industrial automation product portfolio. The artifact should show how portfolio, pricing, lifecycle, NPD, sales enablement, and data-confidence signals can be combined into a defensible roadmap discussion.

## Method

1. Generate synthetic product-family data across technical product platforms and regions.
2. Score each row for transition urgency, pricing confidence, NPD gate readiness, and overall portfolio action priority.
3. Convert the highest-priority records into business-case actions with estimated margin lift, revenue at risk, confidence, owner, next gate, and time to value.
4. Rank NPD concepts by value, investment, payback, evidence, and launch dependency.
5. Surface data-quality controls for the ERP, CRM, supplier, quality, training, and roadmap inputs a PM would need to trust.

## Decision Use

The artifact is designed for a product review meeting where cross-functional stakeholders need to agree on price action, lifecycle transition, migration planning, early NPD gates, and enablement work.
